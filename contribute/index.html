BEFORE YOU JUMP IN

A few things
you might wonder.

Less guesswork. More building.

Do I need professional experience?

You don't need a job title to start. Look for a project whose difficulty and technology match your skills, and read its learning outcomes before joining.

How do I get access to the repositories?

After you join, the project team arranges access to the frontend and backend repositories. You may need to share your GitHub or GitLab account details and accept an invitation. Access may take some time; it is not instant after payment.

What happens after I pick a ticket?

Work on the ticket and submit your contribution for review. Your lead reviews the work and can accept it or request changes. Leads can also assign specific tickets to engineers in the project.

Is team chat included in project membership?

No. Software Engineers can activate the Team Room with a separate ₹199 payment for that project. Buying chat access for one project does not unlock it for other projects.

Can I join as a Tech Lead or Engineering Manager?

Yes, through the separate team portal. Create an account, complete KYC, and wait for admin approval. Once approved, request access to a project and confirm the request. Admin-approved project access for these roles does not require a membership payment.

Does joining guarantee a job?

No. Contribute gives you a place to practice collaboration, work on project tasks, and learn from reviews. It is not an employment contract or a placement guarantee.
